Field guide

From report
to resolution.

  1. Bring a report

    Import findings from your bounty program and preserve the original report ID. Your team can follow each case back to its source.

  2. Read the evidence

    Follow the links between a report, its service, the affected revision, and the available checks in HydraDB.

  3. Review the handoff

    Inspect the suggested owner, then accept or reject the recommendation. Each decision helps rank owners for future cases.
