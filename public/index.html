Review workflow

Graph model →

  1. Map the report

    Match HackerOne or Bugcrowd metadata to the service and revision in your private inventory.

  2. Check staging

    Compare a signed CI manifest with policy. Keep missing observations visible alongside passes and failures.

  3. Assign the review

    Give a person or MCP worker the relevant evidence. Store the outcome so the next reviewer can retrieve it.

Three questions from the review desk.

What actually failed in this case?

The fixture reports 60 days of retention against a 30-day policy. Auditing is enabled. The manifest omits the masking setting, so that check stays inconclusive.

Why suggest a particular owner?

Ichnix starts with the catalog owner. Prior decisions on the same component revision influence the ranking, and a reviewer records the final decision.

What does a coding agent receive?

A named worker claims one assignment over MCP. Its lease grants access to the case context and lets it record a disposition. The console keeps the graph credentials.
